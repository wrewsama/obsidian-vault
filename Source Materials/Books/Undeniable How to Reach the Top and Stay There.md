- Actively seek out suffering. Build confidence from it
- No matter how impossible the dream, keep hammering and find a way
- Focus on your goals. Fear and distractions are the enemy. Repetition is your friend
- Consistently good > Occasionally great
- Crush your impostor syndrome under an undeniable stack of proof that you are who you say you are. Then bet on yourself
- Ignore the opinions of others. Focus on pushing you own limits
- Passion is your willingness to suffer for something. Find something worth suffering for
- Even if you work hard, you will still encounter failures. Own them and keep hammering
- Practice your purpose, live out your purpose, and **leave a legacy of purpose**
