- NOTE: "blogs" are defined as all online published content (including social media)
- news sites get stories from large blogs (e.g. Huffpost), which get their stories from smaller blogs. This opens the possibility of _trading up the chain_
- publishers make money from clicks. Honesty doesn't pay the bills
- tactics
    - give bloggers/influencers free stuff (e.g. products, tickets, etc.)
    - figure out their agenda and give them information pushes that narrative
    - give them what spreads, not what's good; Help them clickbait their readers
