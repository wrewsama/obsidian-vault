## Channel Your Inner Mentalist
- put yourself in the audience's shoes and act accordingly
- don't operate in autopilot, pay attention to every detail
- note down / remember details from old conversations and play the long game. Some connections take years to form, and remembering past conversations help tremendously

## Believe it to achieve it
- visualise in vivid detail
- visualise what the audience is thinking, then work to address it promptly
- visualise possible failures, and plan contingencies

## Make Your Fear of Rejection Magically Disappear
- siloing: separate your self from the self that got rejected
- fast-forwarding: imagine what you would feel about this situation 1 day or 1 year from now

## Focus on Others
- make the audience the star of the show
- questions, active listening, tactical empathy

## Forget Tomorrow Start Today
- set clear, measurable goals, find what you need to do to get them, then start immediately
- purposely delay emotional responses (e.g. giving up)
- don't forget to give yourself credit for your efforts

## Stack the Deck in Your Favour
- Bet on yourself. But always do the work to stack the deck in your favour

## Don't Be Your Own Worst Enemy
- Don't start writing your DNF speech while you're still running: don't self-sabotage
- demand what you're worth

## Ask For Help
- outsource some tasks
- find a mentor, establish a mutally beneficial relationship, then pay it forward to the next generation

## Make Memory Your Superpower
- note everything down in writing
- to remember names: Listen, Repeat, Reply
- train your brain regularly (e.g. with meditation)
