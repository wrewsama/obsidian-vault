## Wealth
- wealth
    - see wealth, not money or status
    - wealth is the assets that earn while you sleep (businesses, assets, etc)
    - understand that wealth can be ethically gained, don't hate wealth
- business
    - own equity, don't rent out your time
    - give society what it wants but doesn't know how to get
    - the internet opened countless untapped opportunities
- allies
    - pick an industry where you can play long term games with long term people
    - play iterated games, let them compound
    - pick business partners with high intelligence, energy, and integrity. Avoid cynics and pessimists
- knowledge
    - specific knowledge: that which cannot be trained
        - what feels like play to you that looks like work to others
        - learned through pursuing genuine passions or apprenticeships
        - techincal or creative
    - skills
        - learn to sell and build
        - there is no "business" skill, avoid business magazines and courses
        - study microecons, game theory, psychology, persuasion, ethics, math, computers
        - reading > listening, doing > watching
- leverage
    - business leverage: from capital, labour, products with no marginal cost of replication (namely, code and media)
    - capital: money, obtained through applying specific knowledge and accountability
    - don't waste your life chasing labour
    - code: an army of robots is freely available, packed in a data centre. Use it
    - media: create books, blogs, videos, podcasts, etc.
- judgement
    - multiplied by your leverage
    - requires experience
    - can be accelerated by learning foundational skills
- time management
    - keep an uncluttered calendar, while being too busy to "do coffee"
    - set and enforce your own hourly rate, then prioritise/outsource/ignore tasks accordingly
- work
    - who you work with and what you work on are the most important
    - still, work as hard as you can
    - don't stop redefining yourself until you become the best in the world at what you do
- no get-rich-quick schemes
- **productise yourself**

> For someone who is early in their career, the single most important thing about a company is the alumni network you're goin to build

> In 1000 parallel universes, you want to be wealthy in 999 of them

Diver parable: Imagine you're the best deep sea diver in the world. By pure luck, someone else discovers a sunken treasure ship too deep for them to reach. Their luck is now your luck. **Become the best at what you do and opportunity will seek you out.**

> Any belief you took in a package (e.g. Democrat, Catholic, American) is suspect and should be re-evaluated from base principles

> If you're evenly split on a difficult decision, take the path more painful in the short term

(if the easy option was the correct one, there wouldn't be a dilemma in the first place)
## Happiness
- happiness is an internal skill that can be learned and practiced
- it requires presence and peace
- get rid of the things you "should" do (for others' / society's expectations), do what you need / want to do

> My number one priority in life, above my happiness, above my family, above my work, is my own health
- we evolved for scarcity but live in abundance. That's why we struggle to say no when our genes always want to say yes
- "I don't have time" is just "It's not a priority". Prioritise your health.
- diet, exercise, meditation

## Philosophy
- Scientific statements must be falsifiable
    - examples: math, microeconomics
    - nonexamples: macroeconomics, religion
- consider basing your philosophy on falsifiable statements only
