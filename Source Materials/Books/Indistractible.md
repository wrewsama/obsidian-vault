> Living the life we want requires not only doing the _right_ things, it also requires that we stop doing the _wrong_ things that take us off-track

- simply removing online technology won't work - you end up replacing one distraction with another

## Internal Triggers
- find and address the root cause of your addiction. It's not the pleasure, it's the pain we're trying to avoid
- to handle distraction, learn to handle discomfort
- observe the sensations you feel before giving in to a craving - this is the internal trigger
- surfing the urge: when you get the urge for distraction, simply delay it by 10 minutes and see if you still want to do it
