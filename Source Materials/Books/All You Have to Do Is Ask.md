## The Law of Giving and Receiving
 1. Give without expectations of return
 2. Give but know your limits
 3. Ask, but don't be too dependent
 4. Take a long-term view, it's okay to be give-heavy or receive-heavy for a while as long as it balances out in the end

## Figuring Out What You Need And Asking For It
#### What You Need
- describe your goals, ensure you state the _why_
- for each goal
    - choose a metric to measure it (OKR)
    - determine what you need to get it
- needs
    - information
    - referrals
    - resources
#### Asking For It
- SMART Requests (not to be confused with SMART goals)
    - Specific
    - Meaningful
    - Action-oriented
    - Realistic
    - Time-bound
- who to ask
    - distant connections
    - dormant connections
    - close connections may not be as effective as their networks are likely similar to yours
- delivery of the request
    - where: consider the audience's preferences (e.g. in-person vs email)
    - when: find a convenient time where the audience can consider their response
    - how: use SMART, be direct and authentic, don't apologise for no reason
