## general principles
- "tidying" should be a process performed all at once, one time
    - beyond that, you only need to put things back where they belong
    - you're meant to _evolve your mindset_, not iterate slowly
- tidy up category-by-category, NOT location-by-location
- discard, then organise
- tidy the categories with the least sentimental value first
- decide specific places for every single item, ensure all items of the same type are in the same place
- keep things simple
## categories
- clothes: fold with the KonMari method and store vertically in drawers
    - arrangement: arrange them such that they "rise to the right" (e.g. heaviest on the left and lightest on the right)
    - note on socks: don't ball them up, just fold and store
- books: consider if you really need to keep the whole book, or just certain important parts. If so, extract them somewhere else and discard the rest
- sentimental items (especially photos):
> No matter how wonderful things used to be, we cannot live in the past. The joy and excitement we feel here and now are more important.

## organisation tips
- arrange storage such that you can immediately see where everything is
    - store things vertically instead of laying them horizontally and stacking them
    - utilise drawers and boxes
- items that usurp floor spae belong in the closet
- keep things dry
    - store bathroom/kitchen items away from the baths/sinks
    - dry and store them after use
- minimise noisy textual labels
