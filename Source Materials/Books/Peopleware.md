## Managing the Human Resource
- even in tech, our major problems are **sociological**, not **technological**
- effectively managing thinking workers
    - error quotas
    - embrace uniqueness
    - a project in steady state is dead, focus on who/what gets things moving
    - ensure people don't just _do_ the task, they should also have time to think, explore, read, etc.
- people under time pressure don't work better; they just work faster
- a policy like "quality --If time permits" will assure that no quality at all sneaks into the product
- set sensibly generous deadlines - or none at all, don't worry about Parkinson's Law
    - in practice, only **organisational busywork** expands to fill the working day
    - longer deadlines doesn't mean slower work

## The Office Environment
- only being able to get work done outside 9-5 is a red flag for the office environment
- key characteristics: quiet and private
- carve out periods of total freedom from interruptions (messages, calls, etc)
- music can be used to block out noise and it's okay for left brain work, but you sacrifice the creative breakthroughs from the right brain
