## Core Maxims
- The person with the most power wins
- Never intentionally offend anyone at work
- Positive relationships build political capital
- All behaviour has a purpose
## Fundamentals
- set goals, not wishes
    - align personal goals with business goals, hurting others for your goals is not worth it
    - pick goals that are actionable by yourself, then focus your attention on working towards them
- **Organisational Facts of Life**
    1. Organisations are not democracies
    2. Some people have more power than others
    3. Virtually all decisions are subjective
    4. Your boss has control over much of your life
    5. Fairness is an impossible goal
- to further your goals, aim to gain leverage
    - Achievements: achieve results
    - Acumen: gain knowledge, especially rare, specialised knowledge
    - Allies: build relationships
    - Alternatives: detach from the situation, gain access to alternatives
- gaining allies
    - identify the people you need to know (i.e. those who you may need to help you achieve your goals)
    - seek opportunities for interaction, be willing to get outside your comfort zone
    - adopt a _consistently_ pleasant personality
    - find shared interests, experiences, or opinions and use those
    - be helpful in general
- handling adversaries
    - convert: align your goals with theirs (and vice versa)
    - contain: increase your leverage over them so they can't deal major damage to you
