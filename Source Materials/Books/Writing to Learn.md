- **learn to write so you can write to learn**
- writing is an exercise in reasoning
- 2 types of nonfiction writing
    - explanatory: to transfer information
        - need to have a clear idea of what we're trying to say
    - exploratory: to figure out what we're trying to say
        - also known as _free writing_
- ways to make writing clearer
    - brevity
    - remove unnecessary jargon / overly verbose nouns
    - use active verbs
    - include visible detail - show don't tell
> The reader has to believe that the writer is feeling good, even if he isn't

- ways to write for learning
    - "report"
        - describe problem
        - discuss approach
        - explain learnings
    - journal
        - keep a running account of the work you've done
        - suspend judgement
