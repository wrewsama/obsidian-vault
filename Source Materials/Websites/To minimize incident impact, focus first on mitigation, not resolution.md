- first goal in incident management: **mitigation**
    - stop customers from being impacted
    - can be done even without fully understanding the root cause
- resolution comes after mitigation is completed
- invest in developing [[Generic Mitigations]]
- beware the temptation to "fix forward" and skip mitigation
    - software always takes longer to develop, test, and deploy than you think it will
    - possibility of introducing new bugs
