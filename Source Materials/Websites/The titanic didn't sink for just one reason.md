- don't tunnel vision on just one simple root cause analysis
    - leads to a convenient illusion of understanding
- explore different perspectives and different layers of the system
- e.g. don't just ask why the titanic sank, also explore:
    - why did it hit the iceberg
    - why it couldn't withstand hitting the iceberg
    - why did so many passengers die
