1. Apologising before you speak
    1. just say the thing directly, with confidence
2. trying to sound smart when someone makes a mistake
    1. don't start lecturing to show off your knowledge
    2. ask the right questions to work towards a resolution
3. merely waiting for your turn to speak
    1. when the other person is talking, listen with your full attention
    2. don't be rehearsing what you want to say next
    3. when you answer, it should relate to what the other person just said, indicating that you were indeed listening
4. filling up silence with filler words
    1. be comfortable with a 3-second silence before answering
5. burying the answer after tons of context
    1. answer the question directly first, then provide the necessary context
6. not doing your homework
    1. prepare for each meeting with responses to likely questions/concerns/objections
