## what
- 4 step decision-making framework: observe, orient, decide, act
- not a perfect loop
    - all phases can link back to Observe
    - can skip Decide (implicit guidance and control: based on strong intuitions from deep expertise)
## steps
- **Observe**: take in information from the world around you
    - unfolding circumstances
    - outside information
    - results of your actions
- **Orient**: use various mental models to interpret the observations and the way they're related to you
    - genetic heritage
    - cultural traditions
    - new information
    - previous experience
    - analyses and synthesis
- **Decide**: come up with options and evaluate the best one
- **Act**: execute the action
